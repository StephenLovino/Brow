What it does

Small app. Big forehead.

Brow lives in your menu bar and does a few things really well. Everything is click-through, so it never gets in your way.

Push everything below the notch

One toggle moves the real menu bar and every window below the camera. macOS does the layout, so maximized and full-screen apps fit too. Quit Brow and your resolution comes right back.

Questions

Good to know

How does "push below the notch" work?

Notched MacBooks also support a display mode that's a little shorter and stops just under the camera. Brow switches to it while it runs, so macOS itself places the menu bar and windows below the notch. It uses a public macOS API, and your normal resolution returns when Brow quits.

Will it block clicks on my menu bar?

No. Everything Brow draws is click-through. In full-width mode you can still click menu bar items under the band, or hold ⌥ to see them.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. The band and widget drawer sit at the top center of your main display. The push-below option only appears on notched MacBooks.

Is it safe to open?

Brow is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other Mac app. You can also read and build the source yourself.

What do I need?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel.
